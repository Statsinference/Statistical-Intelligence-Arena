# AI pre-review and human review record — protocol v0.3

**Template only: no review approval or acceptance has occurred.** AI pre-review identifies issues; scientific signoff and independent reproduction remain human responsibilities.

## AI pre-review checklist

- Exact family ID and target; separate DiD versus synthetic-control subdesign; explicit CATE query and population.
- Verified DGP source locator, original equations/adaptation, rights and version; no invented citation; independent truth check.
- Task-conditional hyperdistribution with role/target/query mapping, constraints, parameter laws and public/held-out separation; no claim that classical source or new seeds proves unseen mechanisms.
- Training plan with honest status, chosen label/teacher, target-specific derivation, optional head meanings, offline budget and checkpoint provenance.
- Fixed-DGP paired repetitions, actual independent unit, complete per-replicate pipeline and frozen CI/nominal/MC rules.
- Matched total labels and allocation; coverage basis/denominator/availability gate; all failures retained; no selective-abstention pass.
- Appropriate classical baseline and native/adapted PFN classification; no estimand substitution.
- Connected/unbounded parameter interval compatibility; disconnected parameter sets need extension; future-response prediction is not the present track.
- Actual implementation, environment and execution evidence support every empirical claim; no fabricated results or approval.
